MISSION BRIEFING

Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance is a 2013 action hack-and-slash game developed by PlatinumGames and Kojima Productions, and published by Konami. A spinoff of the Metal Gear Solid series, the game stars Raiden—the protagonist of Metal Gear Solid 2—who has since become a cyborg ninja and bodyguard for the private military company Maverick. Set in 2018, four years after the events of Metal Gear Solid 4, the story follows Raiden as he seeks revenge against the terrorist organization Desperado, who have assassinated his employers and kidnapped children to be turned into child soldiers. The game marked a radical departure from the series' traditional stealth gameplay, embracing fast-paced combat, sword-based mechanics, and cinematic over-the-top action. Players can slice through enemies and objects in "Blade Mode," slow down time to target specific body parts, and engage in spectacular boss fights against characters like Jetstream Sam and Senator Steven Armstrong. Despite initial controversy over the shift in genre, Revengeance received critical acclaim for its combat system, visual style, and surprisingly deep narrative that explored the psychology of warfare, revenge, and redemption. The game's soundtrack—a fusion of heavy metal and electronic music—became iconic, with tracks like "Rules of Nature" defining the game's identity.

SUMMARY

In 2018, cyborg ninja Raiden fights his way through a global conspiracy involving the terrorist group Desperado and the corrupt Senator Steven Armstrong. After his employer is assassinated and children are kidnapped for cyborg enhancement, Raiden embarks on a brutal quest for revenge. He battles Jetstream Sam, a rival cyborg swordsman, and ultimately confronts Armstrong, who plans to purge the weak and rebuild America through war. The game's narrative explores the cyclical nature of violence and the possibility of redemption, with Raiden ultimately choosing to protect the innocent rather than succumb to vengeance.
