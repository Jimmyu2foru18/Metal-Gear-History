MISSION BRIEFING

Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain is a 2015 action-adventure stealth game developed by Kojima Productions and published by Konami. It is the eleventh installment in the Metal Gear series and the final canonical entry directed by Hideo Kojima. Set in 1984, nine years after the events of Ground Zeroes, the game follows Venom Snake—a body double who assumed Big Boss's identity after the XOF attack on Mother Base. Awakening from a nine-year coma, Venom Snake establishes a new private army, Diamond Dogs, and returns to the battlefield in pursuit of the shadow group XOF. The game features an expansive open-world environment set across Afghanistan and Central Africa, with unprecedented freedom of approach and infiltration. Players build and manage a new Mother Base, recruit elite operatives including the mysterious sniper Quiet, and confront Skull Face and the "Children of the Heavens Drive." The Phantom Pain is widely considered one of the greatest stealth games ever made, celebrated for its refined gameplay mechanics, emotional narrative, and the controversial but brilliant twist that recontextualizes the entire series.

SUMMARY

In 1984, Venom Snake awakens from a nine-year coma and leads Diamond Dogs in a proxy war across the Angola-Zaire border region. He hunts those responsible for the XOF attack on Mother Base, confronts Skull Face, and prevents the activation of a lethal pathogen known as Metal Gear Sahelanthropus. The game's shocking twist reveals that Venom Snake is not the true Big Boss, but a medic who underwent identity rewriting to serve as a body double. This closed loop establishes the legend of Big Boss that Solid Snake will later inherit, creating the philosophical throughline that defines the entire saga.
